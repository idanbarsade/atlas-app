Atlas privacy policy

Atlas is a private, single-user assistant used only by its owner, Idan Barsade.

  • Atlas accesses Google data (Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs) only for its owner's own Google account, and only to perform tasks the owner asks for.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Actions that send or change anything (for example sending an email) require the owner's explicit approval each time.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
